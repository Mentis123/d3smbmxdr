# Marlow Vale · facilitator run sheet

`marlowvale@1`: a seller workshop for the Data#3 Data & AI practice on the Coastwater rails. Six decisions, two screens each (situation and decision, then room discussion), 15 screens, about 40 minutes. Content: `lib/decision-room/marlowvale-content.ts`. Presenter words: `app/decision-room/rails-packs.ts`.

## Run it

When you create the room, choose where the tables are. **In the room**: one device per table, as before. **Online**: everyone joins their table from their own device; the first in holds the pen (option, rationale, confidence, lock) and the rest see the same screen and can **Ask for the pen**. The owner gets a pop-up to hand it over or decline; a quiet owner is replaced after about a minute and a half. Everything else is the same, and the phones show the situation, options and split, so no projector is needed online.

Open `/decision-room/marlow-vale`, choose Facilitate, enter the facilitator key and create a room (one device per table). The private console opens at `/decision-room/marlow-vale/control?room=…`; open the audience display from there. Next moves both screens; Back reviews without reopening votes. Rehearse with no key at `/decision-room/marlow-vale/present`.

## The six decisions

Every option names the Data#3 offer it stands for (shown as a gold tag on the display), using only names already in customer-facing material.

1. Ride the momentum, or earn the roadmap? Copilot Scale Deployment, Copilot Pilot, AI Platform & Agent Governance, AI Envisioning: Copilot & Agents. Funding guidance lives in the notes.
2. Move the reports, or rethink the data? Fabric Foundation Rapid, Analytics Assessment & Design, Data Platform Foundations, AI Envisioning: Azure AI & Fabric.
3. Copilot this month, or Copilot safely? M365 Copilot Readiness Assessment, Copilot Shield, Copilot Pilot, Readiness and Shield together.
4. Business champion, or IT gatekeeper? Data Product Factory, AI Data Readiness, Use Case Assessment, or waiting.
5. Extend the demo, or price production? Agent Prototyping, Iterative Agent Builds, AI Platform & Agent Governance, AI Security & Risk Controls.
6. One phase, or the whole lifecycle? Iterative Agent Builds, Recurring Support Services, AI Squads, AI Managed Service & Support.

Each discussion asks the customer's view first, then what the table gave up, then each table says its opening line to the customer out loud.

Close: read back each table's pattern (licences or services, business or IT, speed or readiness) and what it refused to promise. No scores.

## Discussion

Each discussion opens with the customer's view (who pushes back, what they need to hear), then what the table gave up. Seeing the other side's view is the best-evidenced lever in negotiation research, and buyers mostly stall on fear of choosing wrong, not on competitors. Edited in place on 28 Oct 2026, before any room had run.

## Content changes

Changing a question, option or cost after a room has run needs a new pinned revision (`marlowvale@2`), as Coastwater did, so old votes keep their meaning.
